# Google Business Profile Setup Guide

## Overview
This guide provides step-by-step instructions for creating, verifying, and optimizing your Google Business Profile for Fixnero.

## What is Google Business Profile?

Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) is a free tool that allows businesses to manage their online presence across Google, including Search and Maps. It's crucial for local SEO and helps customers find your business.

## Benefits

- **Appear in Local Search**: Show up in Google Maps and local search results
- **Customer Reviews**: Collect and respond to customer reviews
- **Business Information**: Display hours, location, contact info, and services
- **Photos**: Showcase your work with images
- **Posts**: Share updates, offers, and news
- **Insights**: See how customers find and interact with your listing

4. **Service Area (Optional)**

5. **Contact Information**

1. **Search for Your Business**

2. **Claim It**

## Step 2: Verify Your Business

Google requires verification to ensure you own the business. Common methods:

### Method 1: Postcard Verification (Most Common)

1. **Request Postcard**
   - Google will send a postcard to your business address
   - Takes 5-14 business days to arrive

2. **Enter Verification Code**
   - Once postcard arrives, log into Google Business Profile
   - Enter the 5-digit code from the postcard
   - Click "Verify"

### Method 2: Phone Verification

1. **If Available**
   - Google may offer phone verification
   - Select "Verify by phone"
   - Receive automated call with verification code

2. **Enter Code**
   - Enter code immediately
   - Complete verification

### Method 3: Email Verification

1. **If Available**
   - Select email verification
   - Check business email inbox
   - Click verification link

### Method 4: Instant Verification

If you've verified your business in Google Search Console with the same address, instant verification may be available.

## Step 4: Add Photos

### Photo Guidelines

- **Minimum**: 3 photos
- **Recommended**: 10-20 photos
- **Size**: At least 720px wide, 720px tall
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Quality**: High resolution, well-lit

### Photo Types

1. **Logo** (Required)
   - Upload: FXNR.png
   - Square format preferred

2. **Cover Photo** (Recommended)
   - Landscape orientation
   - Shows business exterior or signature service
   - Use: Hero2.jpg or Hero1.png (cropped)

3. **Interior Photos**
   - Workspace/garage area
   - Equipment
   - Clean, professional environment

4. **Exterior Photos**
   - Building entrance
   - Parking area
   - Signage

5. **Work Examples** (Very Important)
   - Before/after car washing
   - Polished cars
   - Tire work
   - Interior cleaning results

6. **Team Photos** (If Available)
   - Staff members
   - Team at work
   - Professional appearance

### Photo Best Practices

- ✅ Use natural or good lighting
- ✅ High quality images
- ✅ Show actual work and results
- ✅ Update seasonally
- ❌ Avoid stock photos
- ❌ No blurry or dark images
- ❌ No excessive filters

## Troubleshooting

### Verification Issues

**Problem**: Postcard not received
- **Solution**: Request new postcard after 14 days

**Problem**: Address not recognized
- **Solution**: Use address suggestions, add suite/unit number

### Duplicate Listings

If multiple listings exist:
1. Claim all of them
2. Mark duplicates
3. Request merge through Google support

### Suspended Profile

Common reasons:
- Multiple listings for same business
- Keyword stuffing in business name
- Prohibited content in photos

**Solution**: Follow Google's guidelines, request review
