#### 6. Optimize Images (2 hours)
**Why:** Faster website = better Google ranking + happier visitors.

**Large images to compress:**
- `Hero1.png` (5.2MB) → compress to under 1MB
- `FN.png`, `FN1.png`, etc. (1.5MB each) → compress to 300-500KB
- `Asiakas1.jpg`, `Asiakas2.jpg` → compress to under 300KB

**Tools to use:**
- TinyPNG.com (free, easy)
- Squoosh.app (more control)
- ImageOptim (Mac app)

**Steps:**
1. Download image
2. Upload to TinyPNG
3. Download compressed version
4. Replace original on website

## 🆘 Need Help?

### Common Questions

**Q: How do I edit the website?**
A: HTML files can be edited with any text editor (Notepad, VS Code, etc.). Be careful with formatting! Always make a backup first.

**Q: How often should I blog?**
A: Start with 1 article per month. Quality over quantity! Each article should be 1500+ words and actually helpful.

**Q: Should I pay for Google Ads?**
A: Start with free SEO first. After 3 months, if you want faster results, consider Google Ads for your top keywords.

**Q: What if my images are too big to upload?**
A: Use image compression tools (TinyPNG.com). Target file sizes:
- Hero images: under 1MB
- Service images: under 300KB
- Icons: under 100KB

**Q: How do I check if SEO is working?**
A: 
1. Google Search Console - see your rankings
2. Google Analytics - see visitor count
3. Google yourself - search "autohuolto espoo" and look for your site
4. Track bookings - are more coming from Google?
