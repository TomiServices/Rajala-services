# Content Strategy for Fixnero Website

## Overview
This document outlines the content strategy for Fixnero's website and online presence to improve SEO, user engagement, and conversion rates.

## Goals

1. **Increase Organic Traffic**: Rank higher for local automotive service keywords
2. **Improve User Engagement**: Keep visitors on site longer, reduce bounce rate
3. **Drive Conversions**: Increase booking form submissions and phone calls
4. **Build Authority**: Establish Fixnero as the go-to auto service expert in Espoo
5. **Support Local SEO**: Dominate local search results for automotive services

## Target Audience

### Primary Audience
- **Car owners in Espoo and Helsinki area**
- Age: 25-55 years old
- Own or lease a vehicle
- Value quality service and convenience
- Willing to pay for professional automotive care

### Secondary Audience
- Fleet managers (small businesses)
- Car enthusiasts who detail their vehicles
- First-time car owners
- People preparing for vehicle inspection (katsastus)

## Content Pillars

### 1. Service Education
**Goal**: Help customers understand services and their benefits

**Topics**:
- What is ceramic coating and why is it worth it?
- Benefits of professional interior cleaning vs DIY
- How tire hotels save you money and hassle
- When to polish vs when to wax your car
- Understanding different types of car washing

**Formats**:
- Blog articles (500-800 words)
- Before/after photo galleries
- Short video demonstrations (30-60 seconds)
- Infographics comparing services

### 2. Seasonal Content
**Goal**: Capture seasonal search traffic

**Topics**:
- **Fall/Winter** (September-November):
  - When to switch to winter tires (October surge)
  - Preparing your car for winter weather
  - Salt stain removal from car interior
  - Winter car care tips
  
- **Winter/Spring** (February-April):
  - Spring tire change timing
  - Post-winter car cleaning and detailing
  - How to remove winter dirt and grime
  
- **Spring/Summer** (May-August):
  - Summer road trip preparation
  - Protecting your car's paint in summer
  - AC maintenance and interior care in heat
  
- **Year-Round**:
  - Monthly car maintenance checklist
  - Preparing for katsastus (vehicle inspection)

### 3. Local SEO Content
**Goal**: Dominate local search results

**Topics**:
- "Best auto detailing in Espoo"
- "Where to get tires changed in Kivenlahti"
- "Car wash near me Espoo"
- Area guides: "Automotive services in Espoo vs Helsinki"
- Local partnerships and community involvement

**Formats**:
- Location-specific landing pages
- Local area guides
- Customer testimonials from Espoo residents
- Local event participation

### 4. Customer Education
**Goal**: Answer common questions and reduce support queries

**Topics**:
- How to book an appointment
- What to expect during your visit
- Pricing transparency guides
- Service duration estimates
- Payment options and policies
- Parking and directions

**Formats**:
- FAQ pages (already implemented)
- Step-by-step guides with images
- Short "How it works" videos
- Customer journey maps

### 5. Trust Building
**Goal**: Establish credibility and encourage bookings

**Content**:
- Customer reviews and testimonials
- Before/after case studies
- Staff introductions and expertise
- Quality guarantees and warranties
- Certifications and partnerships
- "A day in the life" behind-the-scenes content

## Content Types and Formats

### 1. Blog Articles
**Frequency**: 2-4 per month
**Length**: 500-1000 words
**Purpose**: SEO, education, engagement

**Structure**:
- Catchy title with keyword
- Introduction (problem/question)
- Main content (3-5 sections)
- Visual elements (images, infographics)
- Clear CTA (book appointment)
- Internal links to services

**Example Outline**: "Milloin Vaihtaa Talvirenkaat 2025?"
1. Introduction - Why tire changing time matters
2. Legal requirements in Finland
3. Weather-based guidelines
4. Signs it's time to change
5. Benefits of early booking
6. How Fixnero makes it easy
7. CTA: Book your tire change now

### 2. Service Pages
**Goal**: Detailed, conversion-focused pages

**Must Include**:
- Service description (what, why, how)
- Pricing (transparent, starting from)
- Process (step-by-step)
- Duration estimates
- Before/after photos
- Customer testimonials
- FAQ specific to service
- Strong CTA
- Related services
- Booking form/link

### 3. Visual Content

**Infographics**:

## Conversion Optimization

### Call-to-Action Strategy

**Primary CTAs**:
1. "Varaa aika" (Book appointment) - Most prominent
2. "Soita nyt" (Call now) - Secondary
3. "Kysy lisää" (Ask for more info) - Supporting

**CTA Placement**:
- Above the fold on homepage
- End of each service description
- Within blog articles (contextual)
- Footer of every page
- Sticky mobile button (optional)

**CTA Design**:
- High contrast colors (currently black/grey gradient)
- Clear, action-oriented text
- Large enough for easy clicking
- Mobile-friendly sizing

### Landing Page Optimization

**Elements to Include**:
- Clear headline with keyword
- Compelling sub-headline
- Service benefits (bullet points)
- Social proof (reviews, ratings)
- Pricing transparency
- Visual elements
- Trust indicators (years in business, certifications)
- Simple booking form or prominent CTA
